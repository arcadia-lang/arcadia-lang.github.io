# Gender

Arcadia is not a gendered language.
Gender is not an inherent grammatical property of nouns and does not control agreement.
An unmarked noun does not specify gender.

## Gender Prefixes

Arcadia has two optional gender prefixes:

| Prefix  | Meaning |
| ------- | ------- |
| **pa-** | male    |
| **ma-** | female  |

These prefixes attach to a noun when the intended meaning requires explicit gender.
They function as derivational markers rather than as inflectional markers.
Because they do not alter the word-final vowel, they do not alter the noun's grammatical class.

## Usage

Gender marking may be used when gender is relevant to the distinction being expressed.
Otherwise, the noun remains unmarked.
Gender marking cannot trigger agreement elsewhere in the phrase or clause.
